Private

P2P transactions on the Pirate Chain network are private, ensuring that the sender, recipient, and amount of the transaction are all kept confidential.

Fair

Launched in 2018 with no premine, no dev tax, no ICO, no founders fees, and no central authority. Pirate Chain was built by enthusiasts!

Fungible

Pirate Chain is as private to use as cash, yet works across borders on the internet. No transaction history means coins cannot become tainted.

Secure

The use of zk-SNARKs and dPOW ensures that the integrity of transactions are maintained while the contents are kept private.

Community-driven

The development of Pirate Chain is driven by a dedicated community of volunteers and supporters.

Decentralized

Pirate Chain is a decentralized network, meaning that it is not controlled by any single entity or organization.
