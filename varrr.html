What vARRR makes possible

vARRR is its own blockchain, rather than a token on a shared chain. It inherits Verus features and connects with other PBaaS chains.

Its own Layer 1

Verus PBaaS gives vARRR an independent chain with Verus features, including smart transactions. These serve roles similar to Ethereum smart contracts without some of their drawbacks. PBaaS chains can interoperate with one another.

ARRR ↔ vARRR

vARRR uses AtomicDEX atomic swaps at a 1:1 ratio, with mm2 orders in both directions. This lets holders move funds between Pirate Chain and vARRR. See our Exchanges page for the current AtomicDEX-based wallet.

A route to Ethereum

The Verus–Ethereum Bridge provides provable, decentralized cross-chain communication. It can move value between vARRR and Ethereum without a custodian or a single entity controlling transferred assets.

Mine or stake

vARRR uses 50% proof of work and 50% proof of stake. The proof-of-work side is designed for CPU mining with an ASIC/FPGA-resistant algorithm; staking offers another way to participate.

vARRR splits mining and staking rewards 50/50. Mining reward era 3 follows this schedule:

  • 0.08 vARRR until block 1,125,000
  • 0.002 vARRR until block 6,125,001
  • 0 vARRR thereafter

The primary liquidity basket is vARRR-VRSC-tBTC-Bridge.vETH.

Read more: Verus IDs, staking, and NFTs

Registering a vARRR ID costs 100 vARRR. The referral code PIRATE.vARRR@ gives a 20% ID-registration-fee discount. There are three referral levels.

Only funded *.vARRR@ IDs can stake. Holding funds in an ID offers enhanced security compared with R addresses. .vARRR@ IDs can also create NFTs and trade them on the on-chain marketplace.

One vARRR = 1 ARRR

vARRR is backed 1:1 by 10 million ARRR held in a shielded zs address. The public viewing key lets anyone check the backing reserve.

Read more: launch and pre-conversion

vARRR's 12-day pre-conversion window began in March 2024. Participants' funds stayed locked until vARRR launched on 10 April 2024 as the first PBaaS chain, and they received a 5% protocol discount.

At launch, the protocol distributed an initial 800,000 Bridge.vARRR tokens equally to pre-converters. The pre-conversion guide remains available as a historical record.
