CheetahDEX

Built by Komodo Platform, AtomicDEX was a pioneering atomic-swap-based, cross-chain decentralized exchange. No intermediaries, no middlemen, and no trusted third parties: true peer-to-peer swaps.

“Not your keys, not your coins.”

ARRRtomic swaps were developed for Pirate Chain and integrated into AtomicDEX in 2022, enabling peer-to-peer atomic swaps directly with shielded Pirate Chain wallets. Gleec acquired Komodo's technology, brand, and team in 2025 and continues the work in Gleec DEX.

Our preferred way to use this distributed atomic-swap order book is CheetahDEX, an open-source wallet and DEX that supports ARRR and vARRR.
